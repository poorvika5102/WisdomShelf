The Game of Life and How to Play It

Mastering Spiritual Laws & The Power of the Spoken Word | Florence Scovel Shinn

Foundations & Laws of the Game
Chapters 1 & 2: The Game & The Law of Prosperity
Core Principle Life is not a battle; it is a game of giving and receiving governed by spiritual laws. What you project through imagination and speech will inevitably manifest in your outer experience.
Technique / Practice Spoken Word Command — declaring definite, authoritative affirmations to impress the subconscious mind and align with Divine Supply.
Actionable Rule Expect prosperity as your natural right, and speak only words that align with abundance and immediate supply.
Chapter 3: The Power of the Word
Core Principle Words are dynamic energy; your spoken statements constantly program your subconscious mind and set spiritual forces into motion, returning to you as real-life circumstances.
Technique / Practice Verbal Guarding — actively eliminating idle, self-defeating, joking, or negative expressions from your daily conversation.
Actionable Rule Never state anything about yourself or your situation that you do not wish to see materialized in physical reality.
Universal Laws in Action
Chapters 4 & 5: The Law of Nonresistance & The Law of Karma & Forgiveness
Core Principle What you resist, persists. Resistance to discordant situations creates friction; nonresistance dissolves obstacles and lets the Law of Karma work in your favor through love and forgiveness.
Technique / Practice Blessing the Obstacle — mentally sending peace and goodwill toward difficult people, enemies, or challenges to neutralize negativity.
Actionable Rule Agree with your adversary quickly; yield emotionally to uncomfortable situations to strip them of power over you.
Chapters 6 & 7: Casting the Burden & Love
Core Principle The subconscious is a faithful servant, but carrying worries causes paralysis. Casting your burden onto your "Superconscious" (God within) allows answers to unfold naturally. Pure love is non-possessive and magnetic.
Technique / Practice Burden Casting Affirmation — explicitly handing off anxieties and uncontrollable challenges to the Infinite Intelligence within.
Actionable Rule When feeling overwhelmed, affirm: "I cast this burden on the Christ within, and I go free!" then release all worry.
Divine Plan & Intuitive Guidance
Chapters 8 & 9: Intuition or Guidance & Perfect Self-Expression
Core Principle There is a divine design for every life—the "Divine Pattern." Intuition is the spiritual flash of insight guiding you toward your highest place, purpose, and ideal expression.
Technique / Practice Intuitive Prompt Following — acting immediately on sudden, peaceful inner leads or hunches without letting intellect overthink them.
Actionable Rule Demand your divine blueprint daily by affirming: "Let the Divine Design of my life now manifest in full clarity!"
Chapter 10: Denials and Affirmations
Core Principle A denial neutralizes false beliefs and fears in the mind; an affirmation establishes the truth and draws desired conditions into physical reality.
Technique / Practice Denial & Affirmation Pairing — systematically denying the power of negative appearances followed by affirming divine order and total supply.
Actionable Rule Deny bad news or lack immediately: "There is no reality in this appearance; God is my instant and infinite supply!"

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Cast Your Burdens: Whenever worry strikes, explicitly hand the issue over to Infinite Intelligence and step out of its way.
  • Practice Nonresistance: Bless difficult situations or people instead of fighting them, dissolving opposition with goodwill.
  • Follow Intuitive Hunches: Pay close attention to sudden, calm inner leads and take prompt, positive physical action.
