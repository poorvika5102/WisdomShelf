The Bhagavad Gita

The Eternal Dialogue on Duty, Knowledge, and Self-Realization | Authored by Maharishi Ved Vyasa

Part I: Karma Yoga & Selfless Duty (Chapters 1–6)
Dharma, Action without Attachment & The Steady Mind
Core Principle Your right is to action alone, never to its fruits. Inaction is destructive, but acting out of selfish desire causes bondage; true freedom lies in fulfilling your righteous duty (Dharma) with detachment.
Technique / Practice Nishkama Karma & Sthitaprajna — surrendering expectations of outcome while maintaining an unshakeable, balanced intellect (Sthitaprajna) in both pleasure and pain.
Actionable Rule Perform your essential responsibilities with maximum focus, releasing all anxiety regarding success, failure, praise, or blame.
Part II: Jnana Yoga & Realizing the Eternal Self (Chapters 7–12)
The Imperishable Atman & Cosmic Consciousness
Core Principle The physical body is temporary, but the True Self (Atman) is birthless, deathless, and indestructible. Recognizing the Divine as the underlying essence of all existence destroys ignorance and fear.
Technique / Practice Self-Inquiry & Divine Dedication — distinguishing between the transient physical vessel and the immortal consciousness within, offering all efforts to the Supreme.
Actionable Rule Rise above identification with physical body and ego; recognize the ultimate oneness and divine spark uniting all living beings.
Part III: Bhakti, The Three Gunas & Ultimate Liberation (Chapters 13–18)
Transcending the Gunas & Total Surrender
Core Principle All material nature operates under three qualities (Gunas): Sattva (purity/harmony), Rajas (passion/activity), and Tamas (inertia/darkness). Liberation comes from transcending these modes through devotion and self-mastery.
Technique / Practice Guna Calibration & Sharanagati — consciously cultivating Sattva over Rajas and Tamas, ultimately surrendering the ego to the Divine Will.
Actionable Rule Align daily habits with clarity and truth (Sattva); face difficult decisions through total faith and unreserved commitment to duty.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Execute Duty Without Greed: Focus fully on effort and duty (Dharma) while surrendering outcome anxiety.
  • Maintain Mental Equanimity: Practice emotional balance across triumph, defeat, pleasure, and adversity.
  • Elevate Inner Quality: Choose sattvic, harmonious habits in thoughts, food, speech, and action to transcend lower nature.
