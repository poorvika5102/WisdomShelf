The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

A Guide to Wealth and Happiness | Compiled by Eric Jorgenson

Part I: Building Wealth & Strategic Leverage
Specific Knowledge, Ownership & Permissionless Leverage
Core Principle You don't get rich renting out your time; equity gives you upside. Wealth is built through specific knowledge, high accountability, and maximum leverage (code, media, capital, labor).
Technique / Practice Permissionless Scaling — utilizing modern leverage (software, content, digital assets) that works while you sleep without requiring anyone's permission to build or distribute.
Actionable Rule Productize yourself: combine your authentic interests and unique skills with code or media to build scalably distinct value.
Part II: Judgment, First Principles & Mental Models
Clear Thinking, Radical Honesty & Compound Interest
Core Principle Direction matters far more than speed. Radical clarity, freedom from self-delusion, and understanding fundamental mental models lead to optimal long-term decision making.
Technique / Practice First Principles Deduction & Long-Term Compounding — evaluating problems from basic truths rather than memory/analogy, playing long-term games with long-term people.
Actionable Rule Set a high personal hourly rate; ruthlessly delegate, automate, or ignore tasks below that value threshold to protect judgment and focus.
Part III: Happiness as a Learnable Skill
Peace, Presence & Freedom from Desire
Core Principle Happiness is a state of mind chosen by eliminating desire and conditioning. Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want.
Technique / Practice Desire Single-Tasking & Present Awareness — identifying a single primary desire at a time, removing cognitive noise through daily meditation, and prioritizing physical health above all.
Actionable Rule Treat peace of mind as your default state; build deliberate habits around meditation, exercise, and present-moment awareness.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Acquire Permissionless Leverage: Focus on building software, content, or media products that scale without marginal cost.
  • Play Long-Term Games: Partner exclusively with high-integrity people for multi-decade compound growth in relationships and capital.
  • Minimize Unnecessary Desires: Realize happiness comes from inner stillness rather than relentless external acquisition.
