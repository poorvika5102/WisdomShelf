Make Your Bed

Little Things That Can Change Your Life... And Maybe the World | By Admiral William H. McRaven

Part I: Foundation, Discipline & Teamwork
1. Small Wins, Shared Effort & Heart Over Size
Core Principle Completing the first task of the day reinforces personal discipline and provides momentum. No one succeeds alone; life requires reliable paddle partners, and true capability is measured by character rather than physical appearance.
Technique / Practice Anchor Habits & Support Network Alignment — establishing an unshakeable morning routine to build pride while relying on team synergy during high-stress endeavors.
Actionable Rule Start every day by completing a small, precise task to establish pride and momentum, and actively cultivate trustworthy partnerships.
Part II: Resilience, Fairness & Taking Calculated Risks
2. Overcoming Unfairness, Failure & Calculated Risk
Core Principle Life is inherently unfair, and failures ("sugar cookies") are inevitable. True growth comes from using setbacks as strength-building exercises rather than dwelling on resentment or avoiding necessary risk.
Technique / Practice The "Sugar Cookie" Mindset — acknowledging unfair outcomes without complaint, analyzing failures objectively, and leaning forward into calculated risks to achieve major breakthroughs.
Actionable Rule Refuse to play the victim when life treats you unfairly; view mistakes as lessons designed to build grit for future challenges.
Part III: Mental Toughness, Leadership & Unyielding Hope
3. Standing Tall, Raising Hope & Never Ringing the Bell
Core Principle In your darkest moments—when facing bullies, tragedy, or exhaustion—standing firm and offering hope to those around you changes the entire environment. The only path to permanent failure is quitting.
Technique / Practice Singing in the Mud — consciously projecting composure, optimism, and determination to lift team morale when circumstances are at their absolute worst.
Actionable Rule Be the voice of hope and resilience in times of collective crisis, and eliminate quitting ("ringing the bell") as an option.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Complete Task #1 Daily: Make your bed impeccably every morning to guarantee at least one completed task.
  • Embrace Unfairness Without Complaint: Accept unexpected setbacks gracefully and keep pushing forward.
  • Take Calculated Bold Risks: Step beyond safe boundaries to accomplish meaningful long-term goals.
  • Refuse to Quit: Remove the option of giving up regardless of hardship, obstacle, or emotional exhaustion.
