Do It Today

Overcome Procrastination, Improve Productivity, and Achieve More Meaningful Things | By Darius Foroux

Part I: Understanding & Beating Procrastination
1. The Psychology of Delay & Action Systems
Core Principle Procrastination is not a time-management flaw; it is an emotional and habit failure. Overcoming it requires minimizing friction, eliminating decision fatigue, and relying on repeatable systems rather than unpredictable bursts of motivation.
Technique / Practice Friction Reduction & Minimum Viable Progress — breaking intimidating tasks into tiny, friction-free components so small that starting requires almost zero willpower.
Actionable Rule Never wait to "feel ready"—take immediate, small physical action to generate momentum, trusting motivation to follow.
Part II: Systems for High-Impact Productivity
2. Focus, Eliminate Distractions & Daily Execution
Core Principle Productivity is doing the right things effectively, not filling every hour with busywork. True output comes from protecting deep focus, eliminating low-value tasks, and keeping daily priorities remarkably small.
Technique / Practice The Rule of 3 Priorities — identifying no more than 3 critical tasks per day and completing them before responding to external demands like email or social media.
Actionable Rule Batch administrative chores and aggressively eliminate non-essential commitments to preserve prime energy for priority goals.
Part III: Achieving Meaningful Goals & Long-Term Consistency
3. Compound Consistency & Living Purposefully
Core Principle Long-term success is the compounding effect of small, unremarkable daily efforts repeated consistently. Meaning is derived from taking daily control over your time and aligning activities with personal values.
Technique / Practice Daily Review & System Auditing — conducting a quick evening assessment to track completion, identify time drains, and calibrate tomorrow's system.
Actionable Rule Focus entirely on winning the present day rather than overwhelming yourself with distant, multi-year plans.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Select 3 Daily Priorities: Limit your daily task list to 3 vital outcomes every morning.
  • Lower Starting Friction: Shrink intimidating tasks until starting takes less than two minutes.
  • Eliminate Time Drains: Disable non-essential notifications and batch email checks to fixed times.
  • Review Your Day Nightly: Audit time spent daily to ensure alignment with long-term goals.
