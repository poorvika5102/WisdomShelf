Ask and It Is Given

Learning to Manifest Your Desires | Esther and Jerry Hicks

Core Universal Laws
Part I: The 3-Step Creation Process
Core Principle Manifestation happens in three steps: 1) Ask (done automatically by living life), 2) Answer (done by the Universe instantly), and 3) Allow (your alignment with what you asked for).
Technique / Practice Vibrational Matching — consciously shifting your emotional frequency to match the state of already having received your desire.
Actionable Rule Focus your attention entirely on the presence of what you desire rather than its absence or your current lack.
Part I: The Emotional Guidance System
Core Principle Emotions are real-time feedback indicators showing the degree of alignment or resistance between your physical mind and your Inner Being.
Technique / Practice Emotional Scale Navigation — systematically moving up the 22-level emotional hierarchy (from Fear/Grief up toward Joy/Appreciation).
Actionable Rule Whenever you feel negative emotion, reach for a slightly better-feeling thought to reduce resistance step-by-step.
Practical Processes for Alignment
Part II: Processes for High Alignment (Joy, Love, Appreciation)
Core Principle When you are already in a good-feeling state, engaging in intentional mental games amplifies momentum and accelerates physical manifestation.
Technique / Practice Rampage of Appreciation & The Prosperity Game — focusing intently on things you admire or mentally spending growing sums of money daily to stretch abundance capacity.
Actionable Rule Spend 10–15 minutes daily intentionally listing qualities, items, or people you genuinely appreciate.
Part II: Processes for Moderate Alignment (Hope, Optimism, Neutrality)
Core Principle Neutral or hopeful states are ideal for clarifying goals and scripting new narratives before resistance has a chance to form.
Technique / Practice Segment Intending & Creative Workshop — mentally declaring your desired feeling and outcome before entering a new activity or phase of the day.
Actionable Rule Pause before starting a new segment of your day (e.g., driving, a meeting, entering home) and state your intention for how you want to feel.
Releasing Heavy Resistance & Doubt
Part II: Processes for Heavy Resistance (Anger, Worry, Powerlessness)
Core Principle You cannot leap directly from despair to joy; attempting to do so creates friction. You must move incrementally up the emotional scale.
Technique / Practice Pivot Process & Focus Wheels — identifying unwanted thoughts, asking "What do I want instead?", and gradually writing general thoughts that feel soothing.
Actionable Rule When experiencing a negative event, immediately pivot by asking: "What is it that I now desire as a result of this?"

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Practice Segment Intending: Briefly state your emotional intention before changing activities throughout the day.
  • Use the Pivot Process: Turn negative realizations into instant clarity by focusing on what you prefer instead.
  • Daily Appreciation Rampage: Dedicate a few minutes each morning to actively enjoying and praising what is already going right.
