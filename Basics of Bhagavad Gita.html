Basics of Bhagavad Gita

A Thematic Study of the Bhagavad Gita | Based on the teachings of A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada

Part I: The Five Fundamental Truths (Tattvas)
1. Jiva, Ishvara, Prakriti, Kala & Karma
Core Principle The universe operates on five basic truths: Ishvara (the Supreme Controller), Jiva (the individual soul), Prakriti (material nature), Kala (time), and Karma (action/reaction). The soul is eternal (atman) and distinct from the temporary material body.
Technique / Practice Self-Realization Inquiry — constantly distinguishing between the unchanging eternal observer (soul) and the transient bodily dress influenced by material nature.
Actionable Rule Shift identity away from bodily designations (nation, race, gender) to recognize yourself as an eternal spiritual entity.
Part II: Three Gunas, Karma-Yoga & Detached Action
2. Transcending the Three Gunas through Nishkama Karma
Core Principle All material actions are governed by the three gunas: Sattva (goodness), Rajas (passion), and Tamas (ignorance). Performing duty without attachment to the fruits (Nishkama Karma-yoga) frees one from the cycle of karmic bondage.
Technique / Practice Duty As Sacrifice (Yajna) — offering the results of all daily work, talents, and obligations to the Supreme rather than seeking selfish ego-gratification.
Actionable Rule Execute prescribed daily duties with focus and excellence while surrendering attachment to outcome or personal reward.
Part III: Bhakti-Yoga & Surrender (Ultimate Perfection)
3. Bhakti-Yoga & Complete Surrender (Sharanagati)
Core Principle Bhakti (pure loving devotional service) is the highest ladder step of yoga. Surrendering unto the Supreme (Sharanagati) relieves the soul of fear, bestows liberation (moksha), and re-establishes the soul's constitutional position of eternal loving service.
Technique / Practice Mantra Meditation & Mind Purification — centering the mind through regular chanting and remembrance to cleanse material desires and cultivate steady devotion.
Actionable Rule Incorporate regular spiritual practices into daily life to elevate consciousness above material anxieties.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Practice Non-Attachment: Perform daily duties attentively without being emotionally enslaved by success or failure.
  • Cultivate Goodness (Sattva): Structure your diet, environment, and habits to foster clarity, peace, and elevated thinking.
  • Engage in Daily Meditation: Dedicate focused time each day to mantra meditation to calm and purify the mind.
  • Adopt a Service Mindset: View work and talent as offerings of service rather than purely tools for ego expansion.
