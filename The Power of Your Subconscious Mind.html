Foundations of Mind Power
Chapters 1 & 2: The Treasure House Within & Dual Mind Function
Core Principle Your conscious mind is the reasoning controller; your subconscious is the creative reactor. Whatever belief or concept the conscious mind accepts, the subconscious blindly executes.
Technique / Practice Conscious Guarding — actively filtering negative statements, fears, and doubts before they can take root in your subconscious mind.
Actionable Rule Never use self-defeating language such as "I can't afford it" or "I can't do this"—reframe immediately to positive outcomes.
Chapters 3 & 6: The Miracle-Working Power & Practical Techniques
Core Principle The subconscious responds to vivid mental images fused with feeling. The Law of Reversed Effort states that forced, anxious effort hinders subconscious acceptance.
Technique / Practice Drowsy-State Visualization (Baudouin Technique) — entering a relaxed, sleepy state to repeat a concise phrase or mental scene directly to the subconscious.
Actionable Rule Practice visualization when sleepy right before bed, avoiding force or strain, allowing the idea to sink in effortlessly.
Applied Mind Dynamics
Chapters 4, 5 & 7: Healing, Health & The Subconscious as a Partner
Core Principle Your subconscious constructed your physical body and maintains all involuntary functions. It can heal ailments when supplied with unyielding faith and positive directives.
Technique / Practice Mental Impression Method — silently speaking authoritative commands of health and vitality to your body with full emotional conviction.
Actionable Rule Reframe disease or weakness as temporary disharmony; affirm wholeness and health daily before falling asleep.
Chapters 9, 10 & 11: Wealth, Success & Outstanding Results
Core Principle Wealth is a subconscious conviction. Envy, resentment, or fear regarding money creates a mental block that repels financial abundance.
Technique / Practice Abundance Affirmation — repeating core keywords like "Wealth," "Success," and "Prosperity" quietly to condition the mind toward abundance.
Actionable Rule Rejoice in the financial success of others to eliminate subconscious resentment and open yourself to prosperity.
Overcoming Obstacles & Mastery
Chapters 12-16: Relationships, Forgiveness & Sleep
Core Principle Harmonious external relationships require internal harmony. Unforgiveness and anger poison the subconscious, creating emotional and physical friction.
Technique / Practice Forgiveness Release & Sleeping on Answers — releasing grievances completely while trusting the subconscious to work out solutions during sleep.
Actionable Rule Before sleep, ask your subconscious mind for guidance on specific questions, then wake up expecting intuitive answers.
Chapters 17, 18 & 19: Removing Mental Blocks & Conquering Fear
Core Principle Fear is merely a negative thought in your mind. By replacing fearful images with mental pictures of your desired outcome, fear loses its control.
Technique / Practice Desensitization Visualization — mentally confronting feared situations in a peaceful, safe state until fear dissolves.
Actionable Rule Do the thing you fear, and the death of fear is certain; replace fearful thoughts immediately with mental pictures of success.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Nightly Conditioning: Use the drowsy pre-sleep state to repeat a short, positive statement or image of your primary goal.
  • Guard Mental Input: Immediately correct and reframe negative thoughts or self-limiting language as soon as they arise.
  • Nightly Problem-Solving: Direct your subconscious to solve a specific problem right before falling asleep, looking for intuitive answers the next morning.
