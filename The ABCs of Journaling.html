The ABCs of Journaling

A Creative Guide to Art Journaling, Lettering, and Documenting Life | Abbey Sy

Part I: Foundations of Creative Journaling
An Introduction to Journaling & Choosing Your Medium
Core Principle Journaling is a personal creative playground—there are no strict rules, only possibilities. It serves as a visual and written record of your identity, memories, and artistic growth.
Technique / Practice Material Curation — selecting notebooks (grid, blank, or lined), pens, watercolors, tape, and ephemera that suit your lifestyle and aesthetic preferences.
Actionable Rule Choose a paper weight and notebook style that feels approachable and portable so you can carry it anywhere without fear of making "imperfect" entries.
Part II: Elements of Visual Expression
Hand Lettering, Typography & Color Palette
Core Principle Lettering and color set the emotional tone for a spread. Combining hand-drawn header typography with consistent color choices brings harmony to raw pages.
Technique / Practice Header Lettering & Palette Selection — mixing serif, sans-serif, and script styles for titles, anchored by a 2- to 3-color palette per entry.
Actionable Rule Use bold, hand-lettered headers to establish visual entry points on your page, then fill in details with smaller, neat handwriting.
Ephemera, Collage & Mixed Media Layouts
Core Principle Everyday paper items (tickets, receipts, labels, stamps) hold rich contextual memories and serve as the building blocks for tactile page layouts.
Technique / Practice Layered Collaging — grouping tickets, photographs, leaves, and washi tape off-center to create balance and texture before adding text.
Actionable Rule Collect physical mementos during daily outings and paste them directly into your journal to anchor written memories.
Part III: Journal Types & Creative Consistency
Travel Logs, Everyday Memories & Memory Keeping
Core Principle Creative journaling adapts to any theme—from documenting travel adventures and daily routines to tracking personal goals and favorite media.
Technique / Practice On-the-Go Logging — capturing quick sketches, snippets of dialogue, and time-stamped entries while traveling or during daily errands.
Actionable Rule Dedicate specific spreads to thematic topics, such as travel itineraries, playlist lists, or simple daily highlights.
Overcoming Creative Blocks & Building a Habit
Core Principle Consistency is built on enjoyment, not obligation. Overcoming creative blocks requires letting go of perfectionism and experimenting with creative prompts.
Technique / Practice Prompt-Based Spreads & Quick Doodles — using simple daily prompts or small line drawings to break the fear of a blank page.
Actionable Rule Set aside 10 to 15 minutes daily or weekly to play on paper, focusing on the therapeutic process rather than producing a masterpiece.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Collect Daily Ephemera: Save tickets, receipts, and tags to use as textured backgrounds for your page spreads.
  • Experiment with Hand Lettering: Vary headers with serif, script, and block lettering to create visual focal points.
  • Embrace Imperfection: Focus on the joy of capturing moments rather than maintaining flawless visual aesthetic.
