Autobiography of a Yogi

The Spiritual Classic on Kriya Yoga and Self-Realization | By Paramahansa Yogananda

Part I: Guru-Disciple Lineage & Spiritual Discipline
1. The Role of the Guru & Discipleship Under Swami Sri Yukteswar
Core Principle Self-realization requires guidance from a true master (Guru) who accelerates spiritual evolution. Spiritual training demands rigorous self-discipline, surrender of the ego, and systematic mastery over physical and mental impulses.
Technique / Practice Ego Subjugation & Guru-Disciple Alignment — cultivating implicit trust, receptivity, and surrender to a spiritual guide to neutralize subconscious karmic patterns and mental distortions.
Actionable Rule Approach spiritual development with deep humility and discipline, seeking proven wisdom rather than relying solely on unguided intellectual analysis.
Part II: The Science of Kriya Yoga & Cosmic Laws
2. Kriya Yoga & The Science of Cosmic Energy
Core Principle Kriya Yoga is a psycho-physiological technique that decarbonizes human blood and recharges it with oxygen, directing life energy (prana) around the spine to rapidly accelerate spiritual evolution and transcend cosmic karma.
Technique / Practice Pranayama Energy Control — using precise life-force control and breathwork to quiet the sensory system, calm the heart, and direct consciousness inward toward God-realization.
Actionable Rule Commit to daily, scientific meditation routines to quiet internal sensory turmoil and directly experience cosmic consciousness.
Part III: Unity of Faiths & Bringing Yoga to the West
3. The Unity of All Religions & Global Mission
Core Principle All true religions share an underlying, unified truth: God is the sole Reality, and direct personal experience of the Divine supersedes outward dogma. Western scientific efficiency and Eastern spiritual wisdom must unite for human progress.
Technique / Practice Universal Self-Realization — perceiving the divine light in all beings, serving humanity selflessly, and practicing the presence of God during ordinary daily tasks.
Actionable Rule Harmonize practical material efficiency with daily inward spiritual reflection, viewing all life through the lens of universal divine unity.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Establish Daily Meditation: Reserve quiet time every morning and evening for inward focus and spiritual devotion.
  • Practice Energy Control: Direct your life energy inward through breath control to calm mental chatter and emotional reactivity.
  • Harmonize Material & Spiritual Life: Balance professional duties and practical focus with daily spiritual grounding.
  • Cultivate Universal Perspective: Look past superficial cultural or religious differences to honor the shared divinity in all people.
