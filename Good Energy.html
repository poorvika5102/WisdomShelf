The Surprising Connection Between Metabolism, Science, and Ultimate Health | Dr. Casey Means and Calley Means

Part I: The Metabolic Crisis & Cellular Energy
Mitochondrial Dysfunction as the Root of Disease
Core Principle Most chronic diseases—including diabetes, heart disease, Alzheimer's, and depression—share a common root: metabolic dysfunction caused by impaired cellular energy production in the mitochondria.
Technique / Practice Metabolic Biomarker Tracking — monitoring key blood markers (fasting glucose, fasting insulin, triglycerides, HDL, ApoB, hs-CRP, and HbA1c) rather than relying solely on standard cholesterol panels.
Actionable Rule Order comprehensive metabolic lab work to evaluate optimal, functional reference ranges rather than just "disease-state" standard averages.
Part II: Food as Molecular Information
Unprocessed Foods, Glucose Stability & Gut Integrity
Core Principle Food is not just calories; it is signaling code for your cells. Ultra-processed foods, refined sugars, and seed oils trigger systemic inflammation and mitochondrial stress.
Technique / Practice Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) & Food Sequencing — pairing carbohydrates with protein, fat, and fiber, and eating vegetables/protein before carbs to flatten glucose spikes.
Actionable Rule Eliminate ultra-processed foods and industrial seed oils; focus on whole, nutrient-dense foods that stabilize daily blood sugar curves.
Part III: Lifestyle Signals & Environmental Optimization
Movement, Sleep, Light, and Stress Calibration
Core Principle Mitochondria continuously respond to external environmental signals—circadian light exposure, physical movement, sleep quality, and environmental toxins directly regulate energy output.
Technique / Practice Circadian Alignment & Frequent Movement Cues — seeking morning sunlight within 30 minutes of waking, avoiding blue light at night, and taking short walk breaks after meals.
Actionable Rule Incorporate low-intensity movement throughout the day and protect night sleep to maintain hormonal and metabolic homeostasis.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Track Key Metabolic Biomarkers: Regularly test fasting insulin, HbA1c, triglycerides, and hs-CRP to catch cellular dysfunction early.
  • Eat Unprocessed, Real Foods: Remove refined sugars, white flour, and industrial seed oils from your diet to protect mitochondrial health.
  • Align Circadian Rhythms: Get morning sunlight exposure, take post-meal walks, and prioritize 7–8 hours of uninterrupted sleep.
