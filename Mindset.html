Mindset: The New Psychology of Success

How We Can Learn to Fulfill Our Potential | Carol S. Dweck, Ph.D.

Core Foundations: The Two Mindsets
Chapters 1 & 2: The Mindsets & Inside the Mindsets
Core Principle A Fixed Mindset believes human qualities are carved in stone. A Growth Mindset believes your basic qualities can be cultivated through effort, strategies, and help.
Technique / Practice Self-Talk Reframing — shifting internal dialogue from performance validation ("Will I fail or succeed?") to learning ("What can I learn from this?").
Actionable Rule Add the word "Yet" to any limiting statement (e.g., "I don't know how to do this... yet").
Chapter 3: The Truth About Ability and Accomplishment
Core Principle Effort is not a sign of low intelligence; it is the exact key that transforms potential into actual skill and accomplishment over time.
Technique / Practice Stretch Goal Engagement — deliberately choosing difficult tasks that push you beyond your comfort zone rather than safe, guaranteed wins.
Actionable Rule Evaluate your day by the complexity of challenges embraced rather than the ease of tasks completed.
Applied Domains: Sports, Business & Relationships
Chapters 4 & 5: Sports & Leadership
Core Principle Fixed-mindset leaders demand perfection and create culture driven by ego; growth-mindset leaders cultivate learning, teamwork, and resilience.
Technique / Practice Process-Oriented Leadership — establishing systems that celebrate deliberate practice, continuous improvement, and problem-solving over raw status.
Actionable Rule Encourage constructive feedback and view team mistakes as valuable diagnostic data for growth rather than personal flaws.
Chapter 6: Relationships: Mindsets in Love (or Not)
Core Principle A fixed mindset expects instant, effortless harmony ("If we have to work at it, it wasn't meant to be"). A growth mindset recognizes relationships require mutual development.
Technique / Practice Collaborative Communication — treating relational conflicts as shared problems to solve together rather than judging your partner's character.
Actionable Rule Replace character judgment during disagreements with active collaboration to heal and improve the relationship.
Development & Changing Mindsets
Chapter 7: Parents, Teachers, and Coaches: Where Do Mindsets Come From?
Core Principle Praising intelligence or talent fosters a fragile, fixed mindset. Praising the process (effort, strategy, persistence) builds a resilient growth mindset.
Technique / Practice Process Praise — focusing feedback specifically on strategies used, focus maintained, and hard work applied rather than inherent ability.
Actionable Rule Praise kids, employees, and peers for their choices, strategies, and persistence rather than calling them "smart" or "talented."
Chapter 8: Changing Mindsets: A Workshop
Core Principle Mindset change is a continuous journey of self-awareness. Recognizing your "fixed-mindset triggers" allows you to consciously choose growth.
Technique / Practice The 4-Step Mindset Shift Framework:
1) Embrace your fixed mindset persona.
2) Identify your specific triggers.
3) Name your fixed-mindset persona.
4) Educate and guide it with a growth mindset.
Actionable Rule When facing setbacks or feeling defensive, acknowledge your fixed-mindset reaction and consciously steer yourself toward a growth response.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Practice Process Praise: Compliment effort, focus, and strategy rather than natural intelligence or talent.
  • Use "The Power of Yet": Reframe every temporary failure by adding "yet" to keep the door to learning open.
  • Catch Your Triggers: Identify moments when criticism or failure triggers your defensive fixed-mindset persona, and talk it through with growth-oriented logic.
