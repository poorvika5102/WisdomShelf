Writing Down Your Soul

How to Activate and Access the Extraordinary Power Within | Janet Conner

Part I: The Voice Within & Brain Waves
The Power of Deep Writing & Crossing the Alpha Line
Core Principle Deep writing is not traditional journaling; it is a spiritual practice that bypasses the ego and conscious analytical mind to connect directly with Wisdom/Divine Intelligence.
Technique / Practice Alpha to Theta Wave Transition — writing fast, continuously, and longhand to drop your brainwave frequency into Theta, the gateway to deep intuitive insight.
Actionable Rule Write rapidly without lifting pen from paper or re-reading; speed prevents the inner editor from filtering incoming soul wisdom.
Part II: The Four Steps of Deep Writing
Step 1 & Step 2: Open Up & Inquire
Core Principle Entering deep writing requires establishing a sacred space, setting an intention, and asking raw, specific, penetrating questions rather than generic ones.
Technique / Practice Sacred Opening Ritual & Radical Inquiries — beginning each session with a unifying phrase or breath, followed by big, honest questions written directly on the page.
Actionable Rule Ask real, vulnerable questions on paper (e.g., "What am I afraid to look at right now?") and trust the spontaneous written response.
Step 3 & Step 4: Listen & Appreciate
Core Principle Listening is an active state of receiving non-judgmental guidance. Closing every session with genuine gratitude grounds the insights into physical awareness.
Technique / Practice Receptive Transcription & Thank-You Closing — capturing the inner voice without editing, then concluding with heartfelt appreciation for the guidance received.
Actionable Rule Write down whatever comes through even if it surprises, challenges, or gently redirects your expectations; close with gratitude.
Part III: Practical Application & Spiritual Guidance
Healing, Problem Solving & Navigating Change
Core Principle Deep writing uncovers hidden beliefs, transforms past emotional trauma, and offers creative solutions to complex life challenges by accessing universal guidance.
Technique / Practice Belief Auditing on Paper — exposing underlying limiting beliefs, writing them out explicitly, and allowing the soul's voice to rewrite them with truth.
Actionable Rule When facing confusion or pain, use your notebook to ask: "What is the true lesson here, and what step should I take next?"

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Create a Sacred Writing Space: Choose a quiet location, keep dedicated journals/pens, and use a brief opening ritual.
  • Write Fast to Shift Brainwaves: Move pen across paper rapidly to cross the Alpha line into Theta brainwaves without censoring.
  • Ask Penetrating Questions: Pose direct, honest, and vulnerable queries to receive genuine, transformative soul guidance.
