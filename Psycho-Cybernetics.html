Psycho-Cybernetics

A New Way to Get More Living Out of Life | By Maxwell Maltz, M.D.

Part I: The Self-Image & Your Internal Servo-Mechanism
1. The Self-Image & The Automatic Success Mechanism
Core Principle Your self-image sets the strict boundaries of what you can accomplish; you will never out-perform your self-concept. The subconscious mind acts as a goal-striving cybernetic "servo-mechanism" that automatically guides you toward whatever target image you program into it.
Technique / Practice Mental Rehearsal (Theater of the Mind) — spending 30 minutes daily in vivid, sensory-rich visualization acting out your desired success, allowing the nervous system to process the mental images as real experience.
Actionable Rule Upgrade your self-image intentionally through structured mental visualization rather than attempting to force change solely through willpower.
Part II: De-Hypnotizing False Beliefs & Managing Errors
2. De-Hypnotizing Yourself & The Role of Mistakes
Core Principle Human limitations are largely self-hypnotic beliefs absorbed from past failures or external criticism. The servo-mechanism learns via error correction—mistakes are feedback meant to be forgotten once the trajectory is corrected, not dwelt upon.
Technique / Practice Rational Belief Examination & Error Forgetting — actively challenging limiting assumptions with strict logic ("Is this actually true?") while immediately releasing past failures to store only winning experiences in memory.
Actionable Rule Treat mistakes purely as temporary navigational course corrections, and systematically dismantle self-limiting thoughts using rational analysis.
Part III: Habits of Happiness & Emotional Clear-Space
3. The Success Mechanism vs. Failure Mechanism
Core Principle Personality traits signal whether your success mechanism (Sense of direction, Understanding, Courage, Charity, Esteem, Self-confidence, Self-acceptance) or failure mechanism (Frustration, Aggression, Insecurity, Loneliness, Uncertainty, Resentment, Emptiness) is engaged. Happiness is a practice habit, not a reward.
Technique / Practice Emotional Vaccination & Vacuuming — building emotional tranquility by refusing to react automatically to negative stimuli, and mentally wiping away accumulated daily tension before sleep.
Actionable Rule Practice cheerful, responsive physical habits daily to condition a success-oriented mindset and protect your internal peace.

Master Implementation Checklist

  • Practice "Theater of the Mind": Spend 15–30 minutes daily visualizing yourself executing goals calmly and successfully.
  • Challenge Limiting Assumptions: Rationally question negative self-talk instead of accepting emotional automatic responses as truth.
  • Forget Past Errors: Retain the lesson from a mistake, then instantly wipe the emotional memory of failure.
  • Cultivate Daily Happiness Habits: Smile, act aggressively toward goals, and detach emotionally from petty disturbances.
